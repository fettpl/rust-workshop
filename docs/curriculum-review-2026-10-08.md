## What the shared-borrow milestone implements

The approved unit appends one ownership lesson with two closely related, scaffolded primary families and one delayed review. `shared-count-words` repairs a supplied `String` boundary to `&str` and is intentionally an E0308 compiler boundary. `shared-has-suffix` repairs the owned first argument and its caller reuse, preserving the second shared input; its starter is an E0382 move-use error. The task contracts use typed `&str` calls, direct literals, repeated owner-preservation checks, and a same-owner two-view case. They establish a finite prescribed borrowing contract, not learner-chosen API architecture, general lifetime reasoning, arbitrary transfer, or a no-allocation proof.

The exact-pair criterion `shared-borrow-pair-v1` requires the two distinct current tests-backed primary families. This is stricter than the unchanged global `minimumCodingTasks: 2` policy: an old eligible family plus one fresh family can satisfy the global selector while leaving the new exact pair incomplete. The delayed `review-shared-borrow-delayed-v1` is a separate E0382 `count_lines` recall and does not add a primary family. Delayed selection still uses the existing due and quiet-interval policy, including the more-than-24-hour condition; delayed recall is evidence of reapplication of that bounded rule, not a second primary task.

Assistance remains family-aware. Assistance on a family is retained across its sibling references, and an assisted exact pair can become exhausted without a reset or history rewrite. The Task 3 correction makes coach copy truthful for an actually available counted primary family, a completed pair, exhaustion, no applicable counted family, legacy uncounted practice, and supplementary references. The correction does not create a new recovery policy. Old events, IDs, completion, drafts, timestamps, assistance state, imports/exports, and review identity remain preserved. These contracts and browser state checks do not establish broad ownership mastery, senior or professional readiness, arbitrary learner-source execution, or educational efficacy.

Accepted Task 3 R2 records are source-cut evidence, not a new release gate: 20 registered shared-flow cases, 99 files with 976 unit tests, 5 coach-state cases, and 34 affected ownership browser cases passed with exit 0; the final build and static checks also exited 0.
